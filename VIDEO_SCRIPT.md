## RECORDING TIPS

**Pacing:**
- Speak clearly but energetically
- Don't rush the Kiro demo (0:45-2:00)—that's your differentiator
- Keep gameplay clips short and punchy

**The Money Shot:**
- The live Kiro task execution (around 1:15-1:45) is what will blow judges away
- Show the task description, then show Kiro generating code in real-time
- Emphasize the consistency and quality of generated code

**Energy:**
- Start strong—hook them in 5 seconds
- Build excitement during the Kiro section
- End with impact—make them want to try it

**Technical:**
- Record in 1080p minimum
- Use screen recording software (OBS, QuickTime, etc.)
- Consider picture-in-picture for your face during key moments
- Background music (subtle, upbeat) can help pacing
